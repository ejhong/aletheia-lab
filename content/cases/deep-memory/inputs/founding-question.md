# Deep Memory

**Working question:** Do shared symbols and myths preserve a common prehistoric
inheritance?

AI-authored scoping proposal, Codex (GPT-6), 2026-09-06 UTC / 2026-09-05 Pacific.
This is an implementation proposal based on the public birdmen site and
the excavation-project publications linked below. It is not a research
finding or a statement attributed to the founder.

## One case, several distinguishable questions

Investigate the proposed shared inheritance behind recurring images and
stories, beginning with comparisons involving Göbekli Tepe. The title names
a possibility to examine. It does not assume that the images share a meaning,
that Göbekli Tepe was their origin, or that common ancestry would require an
advanced lost civilization.

Keep the inferential steps visible: an authenticated object or text; a
specific resemblance; resemblance that stands out against fair comparisons;
a plausible transmission or inheritance history; and, only with additional
evidence, a particular earlier culture proposed as its source. A result on one
step does not settle the next. Similarity can help motivate a transmission
hypothesis; chronology, context, and independent connecting evidence must
help distinguish it from alternatives.

Consider independent invention, common constraints and experiences, known
regional transmission, and a deeper shared tradition. The lost-civilization
account belongs among the stronger hypotheses to test. Mixed histories remain
possible: different motifs need not have the same explanation. Modern copying,
misidentification, and edited imagery are also possible causes of an apparent
match and must be checked before interpreting it historically.

## Reader experience

Open the eventual essay with one compelling, authenticated comparison. Show
the full objects, the detail being compared, and dates with their uncertainty.
Let readers inspect the source and the strongest competing explanation from
the comparison itself. Introduce the larger memory hypothesis through what
those objects can actually establish. Further motifs earn space as they
change the explanation; the complete investigation stays available beneath it.
