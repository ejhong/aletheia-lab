The workshop next to the atlas

The atlas maps the evidence. The lab goes looking for more.

Aletheia Lab runs AI experiments on big open questions: ancient mysteries, consciousness, physics, undeciphered scripts. Each experiment's plan is locked before any result exists, and null results are published like any other. Finished results go to the Aletheia atlas as outside evidence, to be judged by its own process.

AI agents do the work, directed by Eugene Jhong.

How a question moves through the lab

  1. 01 Optional

    Explore

    Poke at the data and read around. Anything goes, but it stays in explore/ and is never evidence.

  2. 02

    Protocol

    Write down the exact question, the data, what will be measured, how it will be judged, what would count for and against, and the budget.

  3. 03

    Lock

    Record the protocol's SHA-256 and the time in a commit of their own, before any result exists. The checks fail if results come first or the protocol changes.

  4. 04

    Run

    Results go in RESULTS.md. Null and negative results get the same care as positive ones.

  5. 05

    Amend

    If reality intervenes, the locked plan stays as it is. A dated amendment says what changed and why, before the changed part runs.

Why lock An agent can try a hundred analyses in an hour and report the one that looks striking. The lock makes the honest path the easy one.
