Does predicted tubulin binding follow clinical & in-vivo anaesthetic potency?

Across 27 volatile anaesthetics, 1JFF best-site affinity gives Spearman ρ = +0.29 (p = 0.146; 4I4T: ρ = +0.35; Vinardo: ρ = +0.28). Controlling for heavy-atom count gives partial ρ = +0.44 (p = 0.027), and controlling for olive-oil solubility gives partial ρ = +0.16 (p = 0.439).

Non-anaesthetic test

Does tubulin reject fat-soluble non-immobilizers (F6, F8, TFMB, cyclooctane, perfluorocarbons)?

Raw ROC AUC for binding true anaesthetics tighter than the 7 confirmed non-immobilizers is 0.21 (p = 0.017; median −ΔG is 5.50 kcal/mol for anaesthetics vs 6.05 kcal/mol for non-immobilizers). Residual relative to the olive-oil trend is +1.34 kcal/mol (p = 0.007); relative to heavy-atom size it is −0.76 kcal/mol (p < 0.001).
