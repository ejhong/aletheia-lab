Does the model reproduce the 613 THz downward-vs-upward sign split on the original 10 gases?

Physics check: 3D rotational symmetry

Does the published 2017 Hamiltonian give the same answer when the protein is rotated in empty space?

No. Discarding off-diagonal tensor terms breaks 3D rotational symmetry: rotating tubulin rigidly across 20 random orientations swings isoflurane's shift 27-fold (37 to 1018 GHz) and flips TFMB's sign (−3703 to +2722 GHz). Fixing the symmetry (full 3N × 3N Drude model) drifts by 10⁻⁹ GHz and still finds no tubulin potency tracking (ρ = −0.12).
