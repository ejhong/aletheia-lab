# Review of the plan before it was locked

Two models from other vendors read `protocol.md` before any ammonium result existed: OpenAI `gpt-6.2-astra` and
Google `gemini-4-neon`, on 2026-11-23. Their full replies are in `round1/` and `round2/`, with the text they saw.
Cost: $1.78 at list price.

## Round 1

The first plan scored the largest 20-year mean in the 200 years after each onset.

| Point (who) | Done |
|---|---|
| A single peak is not a century-long rise (both) | The deciding measure is now D: years 40 to 160 after the onset against the 100 years before. The peak (P) and level (R) are reported. A fall-back measure (H) was added. |
| Quiet times are not a null for cooling onsets, and a binomial test on them is unsupported (both) | The binomial test is dropped. Quiet times only show how much the measure wanders. A nearby-reference variant is reported. |
| "In both cores" did not require the same events to pass (OpenAI) | The paired outcome (both cores, one, neither) is reported. |
| "Not seen" could mean low sensitivity (both) | A sensitivity figure is reported: how large a rise would reach the top quarter. |
| Rank 1 is fragile and blind to size (both) | The Younger Dryas's D is reported beside the largest and median of the others; the two verdicts are kept separate. |
| The measure is not immune to a misplaced onset (both) | Runs with every other onset moved by 20 and 40 years each way are reported. |
| Dust is a check on the program and dates, not on sensitivity to ammonium (OpenAI) | Stated. Nitrate and sodium added as further context (Google). |
| "Fixed to 4 years" misreads the source; universal statements about dust and snowfall (OpenAI) | Reworded. |
| Conclusions about "sources" exceed the data (OpenAI) | Conclusions are about ammonium concentration in the ice. |
| Ambiguities: rounding, non-positive values, events that cannot be evaluated, ties (both) | Spelled out. |

Not done: made-up pulses passed through the pipeline (the sensitivity figure stands in for them); climate-matched
pseudo-onsets; correcting for snowfall; a coarser averaging scale.

## Round 2: errors only

Fixed before the lock: a percentile in the top quarter did not require D to be above zero (OpenAI); percentiles
for P and R must use the quiet times where those are defined (both); "not among the largest" swallowed the case
where the Younger Dryas cannot be evaluated (OpenAI); P was described as a ratio of means (both); ages of the
extra events are not on the 10-year grid (both); the joint verdict spoke of a "clear margin" that no rule defined
(OpenAI); a claim about which way smoothing pushes the ranking (OpenAI); the statement of what had been seen
(OpenAI).

There was no third round. The plan as locked has not been read by another model.
