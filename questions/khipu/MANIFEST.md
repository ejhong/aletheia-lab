### Published Primary Historical Benchmark (External Colonial Document)
- **1670 San Pedro de Corongo *Revisita* (Santa Valley, Peru):** Transcribed by Zevallos Quiñones (1991), *Etnohistoria del área virú-santa* (Trujillo), and cited in Urton (2017, *Inka History in Knots*, pp. 217–239), Medrano & Urton (2018, *Ethnohistory* 65:1–23), and FitzPatrick (2024, *Ethnohistory* 71:443–470): `132` total tributaries (`130` named in the *padrón* across `6` *pachacas*: `Namus = 18`, `Corongo = 23`, `Cuyuchin = 9`, `Cusca = 7`, `Guauyan = 41`, `Ucore = 32`), total tribute assessed `367` pesos (`1/4` real, `1` quartillo; `2` pesos `7` reales `3` quartillos per tributary).
- **Digitization note:** Outside this published 1670 *revisita* benchmark, no open machine-readable corpus of 16th–17th century Andean colonial *visitas* / *revisitas* is publicly downloadable; this study therefore pairs a full audit and false-positive calibration of the 1670 Santa Valley match with a **corpus-wide internal Rosetta hunt** across all `619` khipus in `khipu.db` (`54,403` cords, `110,677` knots) and a provenance-level census of Rosetta-eligible khipu archives.
