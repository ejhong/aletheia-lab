## Published literature reference end-members (cited in `protocol.md` & `analysis/pt_fingerprint.py`)

- **CI Chondrite HSE normalization & end-member:** Wasson & Kallemeyn (1988), *Phil. Trans. R. Soc. Lond. A* 325:535–544 & Horan et al. (2003), *Chem. Geol.* 196:5–20 (cross-verified against `data/sun2020_sm.pdf` Table S3): `Os = 490 ppb, Ir = 460 ppb, Ru = 710 ppb, Pt = 990 ppb, Pd = 560 ppb, Re = 37 ppb`; `¹⁸⁷Os/¹⁸⁸Os = 0.126` (Walker et al. 2002).
- **Niuatahi-Motutahi submarine volcanic sulfur condensates (Tonga rear arc):** Park et al. (2016), *Geochimica et Cosmochimica Acta* 174:236–246, Table 1 ranges (cited in `data/green2025_pmc.xml`): `Pd = 0.379–0.710 ppb` (mid `0.545`), `Pt = 0.414–0.780 ppb` (mid `0.597`), `Ru = 0.012–0.041 ppb` (mid `0.0265`), `Ir = 0.0003–0.0007 ppb` (mid `0.0005`), `Re = 10.8–16.0 ppb` (mid `13.4`).
- **GISP2 Greenland ice core Pt/Ir peak (Petaev et al. 2013, *PNAS* 110:12917–12920; cross-verified in Wolbach et al. 2018 & Green et al. 2025 `data/green2025_pmc.xml`):** Peak `Pt = 82.2 ppt`, `Pt/Ir` spans `~100–1264` across the 14-year anomaly (Petaev et al. 2013 abstract/text; used strictly for cross-site `Pt/Ir` context in Pillar 1B, not in full 6-HSE Pillar 1A).
- **Upper Continental Crust (UCC) `¹⁸⁷Os/¹⁸⁸Os`:** `1.26–1.40` (Esser & Turekian 1993; Peucker-Ehrenbrink & Jahn 2001, *G³* 2:1061); 6-HSE abundances taken directly from `data/sun2020_sm.pdf` Table S2 (`Os = 31 ppt, Ir = 22 ppt, Ru = 30 ppt, Pt = 510 ppt, Pd = 520 ppt, Re = 198 ppt`).
