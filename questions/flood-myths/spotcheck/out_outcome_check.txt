== c2 (n_groups=137) | Deluge and conflagration combined | RU: Потоп и пожар
   World is destroyed once with fire or draught, another time with a flood or it is destroyed with a flood of fire or boiling water

== c4 (n_groups=40) | The flood: fruits fall from a tree | RU: Потоп: падающие плоды
   During the flood or in the beginning of times fruits, seeds or other objects are dropped into water one by one, usually by a person who has climbed a tree. As far as the objects fall, water recedes and the dry land appears

== c7 (n_groups=30) | The flood: breaking the dam | RU: Потоп: разрушенная преграда
   In the beginning of times or after the flood a dam of earth, person or creature does not let water to recede. The dam is broken or opened, waters withdraw

== c9a (n_groups=4) | The flood: people turn into toads | RU: Потоп: люди превращаются в жаб
   During a flood or when crossing a river those people who drown (or those who survive) turn into toads

== c10 (n_groups=24) | The flood: the wet tails | RU: Потоп: мокрые хвосты
   During the flood birds or animals escape to some place (mountain, tree, boat, sky). Tips of their tails or other body parts get covered with water or foam and acquire their present color and form

== c10a (n_groups=10) | The flood: birds cling to the sky | RU: Птицы цепляются за небо
   During the flood some birds survive clinging to the sky with their beaks

== c12a (n_groups=6) | The dog and the world cataclysm | RU: Потоп и собака
   A dog warns people about approaching flood or world conflagration, instructs them what should be done

== c12b (n_groups=26) | People descend from a dog | RU: Люди – потомки собаки
   In the beginning of time or after the flood man marries a bitch. Present people descend from them.

== c13 (n_groups=35) | The objects’ revolt | RU: Бунт вещей
   During or before the world cataclysm (deluge, darkness) or (Suruí) in particular place at night, household objects and/or stones, trees, domestic animals turn into wild beasts and monsters

== c14 (n_groups=59) | Monsters destroy people | RU: Чудовища уничтожают людей
   During or before the world catastrophe (deluge, darkness) wild beasts and monsters attack and destroy people

== b7b (n_groups=36) | Waters from broken pot | RU: Вода из сосуда
   Sea, river, waters of flood flow out of a small container

== b7d (n_groups=5) | Water flows from broken bone | RU: Вода льется из косточки
   Water flows from a broken bone (of a bird) producing a flood

== b52a (n_groups=14) | Vulture creates the landscape | RU: Стервятник создает сушу
   Flying above the earth and flapping its wings, a bird (usually a vulture) makes it dry after the flood or otherwise creates present landscape

== g9a (n_groups=12) | Restored forest and flood | RU: Срубленные лес и потоп
   A man fells trees to clear ground for a garden. Certain person or animal revives vegetation and after being surprised by the man, warns him about the coming flood, tells to prepare a boat

== i81a (n_groups=15) | The chthonic crab | RU: Хтонический краб
   A giant crab produces earthquakes or floods, stops or can stop flow of the waters into the chasm or on the contrary prevents waters from being dammed.

== k38d (n_groups=77) | Monster blocks waters | RU: Змей закрывает воду
   A monster blocks sources of water (or sends floods) and usually gives some (promises not to send floods) in exchange for human victims or valuables. Hero kills the monster

== l28a (n_groups=14) | Eating of snake meat triggers flood | RU: Съевший змею вызывает потоп
   A river or lake appear or a flood begins because certain person ate meat of a snake or a strange fish

== c3 (n_groups=28) | Snakes stops up a hole in the Noah’s arch | RU: Змея заткнула дыру в ковчеге
   When a hole is opened in the arch, a snake (eel, frog) stops it up with its own body

== c5a (n_groups=124) | Bird-scouts | RU: Птицы-разведчики
