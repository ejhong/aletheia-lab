# A continuing research conversation

Research-update rehearsal · 7 September 2026 · Aletheia Lab

Prepared for the founder and reviewers by GPT-6 (OpenAI Codex), using interactive
research and source checks. This is a working experiment record. The proposed
content has not received independent ratification.

The central product test is whether returning to a topic produces a better
article and a richer, more reliable record beneath it. This rehearsal takes
the founder's ordinary practice—discuss a new development or an idea with an
AI, investigate it, then revise an article—as the model for automation.

## One process, different starting points

Casting is the primary update test. It already has an article, arguments,
sources and an extensive claim catalogue. Deep Memory is the incomplete-case
test: a small ledger should not cause the researcher to forget the broad
question that motivated it. Starting a case can remain a conversation using
the existing scaffold; the same researcher then improves it.

The researcher receives the founding texts, current article and assessment,
ledger, inbox context, earlier research and decisions. It investigates what
could improve understanding, including old evidence reconsidered for a good
reason. Its result should explain the topic and recommend useful changes,
with enough depth for readers who want to investigate further.

The editor receives that whole result with its original context and citations.
It checks consequential readings, reconciles the records, and prepares the
illustrated account and assessment for independent review. One better paragraph
can be a worthwhile outcome. A substantial reframing can warrant a rewrite.
Neither more records nor a changed verdict is necessary.

## What the implementation now makes inspectable

`node scripts/research-case.ts <case> --prepare` saves the exact instructions
and input, plus an inventory, without a model call, spending reservation or
intake event. It uses the same packet builder as the manual research runner.
Full founding texts and the current ledger fit in the present packet bound;
oversize fails rather than silently discarding context.

Where the previous report's input is available, the packet identifies added,
changed and removed records. An older provider's missing or incompatible input
is labeled unknown history. This comparison helps navigation; it is not an
importance score or evidence of what a previous model actually read.

On completion the runner writes `handoff.json`, binding the original request
to the complete provider response and citation annotations. This addresses the
specific omission in the earlier Gemini-to-editor trial. Selected findings,
corrections and deferred leads use the existing intake memory, with reasons for
reconsideration. The case schema and publication gates are unchanged.

## What remains to be demonstrated

The seven targeted research tests pass: they cover intact founding inputs and
citations, preparation without a model call or intake mutation, record changes,
unknown historical inputs, oversized packets and existing rest behavior.
The proposed ledger bundles and casting edition have also passed the production
loader and their proposal validators. The full suite passed all 374 tests; lint, TypeScript and the production
export also passed. Internal links across 584 pages resolved. Visual inspection
sampled the casting opening, article and assessment at desktop and phone widths;
the emulated phone viewport had no horizontal overflow and all five images
(cover plus four plates) loaded. These checks do not constitute independent
epistemic review.

This was an interactive rehearsal. It used no additional repository model API
calls. The runner still uses the configured OpenAI transport, whose earlier
account availability failed; preparing a packet does not fix provider access.
Paid automation remains paused. The completed earlier Gemini trial remains a
separate experiment with its own costs and limitations.

The next product experiment is to commission a researcher on the existing
casting account with this memory, carry its complete result through editing,
and compare the proposed edition with the incumbent. Deep Memory tests the
same process against an unusually incomplete account. A subsequent pass should
demonstrate that corrections and deferred questions influence the work, without
requiring rediscovery or a fresh essay every time. This rehearsal does not yet
establish autonomous repeated improvement, broad Deep Memory coverage, provider
superiority or saturation.

Deep research should become less frequent when verified improvements diminish.
New inbox material, important unresolved questions and occasional reconsideration
can provide reasons to return. A model's claim of novelty or saturation is not
enough to justify another paid investigation.
